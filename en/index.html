Frequently asked questions

Where are my notes stored? Will deleting the app lose them?

Your notes are ordinary .md files inside the folder you picked — the app keeps no hidden copy. Deleting the app does not delete them; reinstall and pick the same folder.

How do I get started?

Tap “Choose folder” and pick one in the system document picker (iCloud Drive, On My iPhone, or any location shared by another app). That folder becomes your library.

Does it need the internet?

No. The app makes no network requests and has no accounts, subscriptions or analytics. Everything works in airplane mode.

Which formats are supported?

Native editing: Markdown (.md / .markdown / .txt). Viewing: PDF, images, Word, CSV and other common files; CSV tables can be edited in-app.

How do I move to a new phone?

If the folder lives in iCloud Drive, install the app and pick the same folder. Otherwise copy it with the Files app first.

Where do I change themes, fonts and the toolbar?

The theme button on the home screen offers 10 palettes (one light and one dark each). The gear at the bottom of the editor adjusts fonts, line spacing and toolbar buttons.

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email haohao8653@126.com with your device model, iOS version and steps to reproduce. Screenshots help a lot for display issues.
