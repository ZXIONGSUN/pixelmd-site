Pixel MD 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 26 日 · 适用于 Pixel MD iOS 版

一句话：Pixel MD 不收集你的任何数据。App 完全离线运行——没有账号、没有服务器、没有统计、没有广告、没有第三方 SDK，不发起任何网络请求。你的笔记始终只存在于你自己选择的文件夹里。

1. App 接触的数据

  • 你的笔记文件：你在系统文件选择器里指定的文件夹中的文件。App 只在本机读取和修改这些文件，用于编辑、预览和导出，不会副本另存、不会上传。
  • App 设置：主题、字体、工具栏、排序方式等偏好，保存在设备本机的 App 设置里，卸载 App 时一并清除。
  • 索引与缓存：为加快列表加载而在内存或本机临时生成的索引，不离开设备。

2. 网络

Pixel MD 不发起任何网络请求，飞行模式下功能完整。App 内不包含广告、分析、崩溃上报或任何第三方 SDK。

以下系统功能由 iOS 提供，数据不经过我们：iCloud 云盘的同步由系统负责（如果文件夹在 iCloud 云盘里）；听写使用系统自带的本机识别模型。

3. 权限说明

  • 文件访问：只访问你主动选择的文件夹；你可以随时在「文件」App 或 App 内切换文件夹。
  • 相机：拍下纸上的文字，用系统自带能力在本机识别成笔记；照片不上传、不保存到相册。
  • 麦克风与语音识别：语音输入使用系统的本机听写模型（iOS 26 及以上）；识别在你的设备上完成。
  • 面容 ID：用于本机解锁 App，验证由系统完成，我们不接触任何生物识别数据。

4. 我们不会做的事

  • 不收集、不上传、不出售、不共享你的笔记或个人信息；
  • 不建立用户画像，不做跨 App 或跨网站追踪；
  • 不使用崩溃收集、行为统计或广告 SDK。

5. 儿童隐私

App 不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。App 内无账号、无社交、无广告。

6. 数据的删除

你的笔记由你完全掌控：在「文件」App 或 App 内删除文件即可；卸载 App 会清除 App 设置和缓存，但不会删除你文件夹里的笔记文件。

7. 政策更新

如果未来版本改变数据处理方式，会先更新本页并修改生效日期。由于 App 的设计约束是零联网，我们不会加入收集数据的组件。

8. 联系我们

关于隐私的任何问题：haohao8653@126.com。

Privacy Policy (English)

Effective: November 26, 2026 · Pixel MD for iOS

In one sentence: Pixel MD collects nothing. The app runs entirely offline — no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs, and no network requests. Your notes always live only in the folder you chose.

1. Data the app touches

  • Your note files: files in the folder you pick with the system document picker. They are read and written on your device only, for editing, preview and export. No copy is kept elsewhere and nothing is uploaded.
  • App settings: theme, font, toolbar and sort preferences, stored locally in the app's settings and removed when you delete the app.
  • Indexes and caches: temporary in-memory or on-device data used to speed up the library list. They never leave the device.

2. Network

Pixel MD makes no network requests and works fully in airplane mode. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or third-party SDKs.

These system features are provided by iOS and do not involve us: iCloud Drive syncing (if your folder lives in iCloud Drive) is handled by the system; dictation uses Apple's on-device recognition model.

3. Permissions

  • File access: only the folder you explicitly choose; you can switch folders at any time.
  • Camera: scans printed text and recognizes it on-device. Photos are not uploaded or saved to your library.
  • Microphone and speech: voice input uses the system's on-device dictation model (iOS 26 and later). Recognition happens on your device.
  • Face ID: used to unlock the app locally. Authentication is performed by the system; we never access biometric data.

4. What we never do

  • We do not collect, upload, sell or share your notes or personal information;
  • We do not build profiles or track you across apps or websites;
  • We do not ship crash reporting, behavioral analytics or advertising SDKs.

5. Children's privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. There are no accounts, no social features and no ads.

6. Deleting your data

You control your notes completely: delete files in the Files app or in Pixel MD. Deleting the app removes its settings and caches but does not delete your note files.

7. Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated first and the effective date revised. Because zero network access is a design constraint, we will not add data-collecting components.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: haohao8653@126.com.
